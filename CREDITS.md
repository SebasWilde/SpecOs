## About SpecOS

SpecOS emerged from real-world experience leading product teams adopting
AI agents in their development workflow. It addresses a gap no existing
methodology covered: how humans and agents collaborate with a single
source of truth, structured specs, and configurable outputs — without
vendor lock-in or heavyweight tooling.
